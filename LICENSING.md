# Licensing, in plain words

Segreant is published under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE) with an
Additional Use Grant. The LICENSE file is the binding text; this page explains
what it means.

## What you can do, free

- **Use it for anything**, as a person or as a company: on your own projects,
  at work, on paid client work, across your whole organization, with a team
  server for your own staff.
- **Change it, share it and contribute** to it.
- **Build your own product on it and sell that product**, as long as your
  product is more than Segreant under another name.

No account, key, payment or permission is needed for any of that.

## The two things you may not do

1. **Offer Segreant as a service.** You may not run it, or a modified version
   of it, as a hosted or managed service for other people or other companies.
   Running it for your own organization is fine.
2. **Sell Segreant itself.** You may not sell, rent or charge a licence fee for
   Segreant or a modified version of it, including a copy relabelled under
   another name.

## Every version becomes fully open source

Two years after a version of Segreant is first published, that version
switches to the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0),
an Open Source license, and both limits above end for it. Each later version
follows on its own two-year clock.

Until then, Segreant is *source-available*, not *open source* in the strict
(OSI) sense. We say so plainly rather than borrow a label it has not earned yet.

## Why these terms

The goal of Segreant is to move AI from paying for tokens to paying for value,
and that needs as many people and organizations as possible measuring their own
AI work, building on the measurement and improving it. The only thing these
terms hold back is someone else turning this project into a paid service or a
resold copy before it is fully open.

## Something else in mind

If you want to do something the two limits forbid, or you need different
terms, open a
[discussion](https://github.com/GamingDragonwastaken/Segreant/discussions).
